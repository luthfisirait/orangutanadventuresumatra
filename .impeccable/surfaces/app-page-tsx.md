Scope: `app/page.tsx` and the shared `HomeContent` homepage surface.

Mode: Persuade.

Audience: International travelers, especially Europeans, comparing Bukit Lawang orangutan trek options before arriving in Indonesia.

Job: Understand the offer, trust the local guide, compare the right trek format, prepare responsibly, and start a booking or WhatsApp conversation.

Primary action: Review trek options and continue to booking. Secondary action: Ask a direct question on WhatsApp.

Proof: Real project photography, published package details and prices, guide identity, practical itinerary detail, ethical wildlife guidance, guest reviews, and direct Bukit Lawang contact channels.

Constraints: Preserve localized content, SEO structure, factual claims, booking and deposit flow, analytics, and the statement that wildlife sightings are not guaranteed.

Chosen direction: Forest Transect. The homepage behaves like a field route from trailhead to habitat, camp, river return, and booking. High-visibility survey markers and strong rules orient the visitor while documentary photography carries emotion.

Memorable moment: The first viewport places the brand and offer over a real wide jungle approach, then a route rail visibly continues into the package comparison below.

Unresolved decisions: Final token values and exact responsive breakpoints will be confirmed during implementation and browser review.
