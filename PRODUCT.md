# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are international travelers, especially visitors from Europe, who are planning an orangutan trek and wider stay in Bukit Lawang, North Sumatra. They need to compare trek formats, understand practical and ethical considerations, build confidence in the local operator, and make a booking or contact the team from abroad.

## Product Purpose

Orangutan Adventure Sumatra helps travelers choose and book guided Bukit Lawang jungle treks, private trips, and local activities. Success means visitors can understand the experience, select an appropriate package, prepare responsibly, and move into a clear booking conversation or deposit flow.

## Positioning

The product presents a locally operated, guide-led experience built around personal service, transparent trip information, ethical wildlife encounters, and practical benefit for the Bukit Lawang community and conservation context.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly evaluate the site before arriving in Indonesia and may be planning around international flights, transfers from Medan or Kuala Namu, accommodation, weather, physical ability, family needs, and limited connectivity or payment options while traveling. WhatsApp is a primary conversation channel. The site also supports direct booking, deposit information, trip comparison, preparation guidance, and multilingual research.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing English, German, French, and Dutch experiences.
- Preserve trek packages, published pricing, factual inclusions, booking and deposit functionality, WhatsApp contact, email, analytics, structured data, and SEO routes.
- Preserve the existing Next.js implementation and responsive web delivery.
- Wildlife sightings must not be presented as guaranteed.
- Exact conservation allocations, outcomes, credentials, availability, and other proof must not be invented; use only verified content already present in the project.
- The interface must work well for international visitors on mobile and desktop, including users making decisions from limited or unfamiliar travel contexts.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Orangutan Adventure Sumatra name and existing logo assets. The brand should remain local, personal, trustworthy, nature-conscious, adventurous, and respectful of wildlife rather than feeling like a generic mass-tour operator.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing brand marks: `public/images/logo.svg`, `public/images/logo-mark.svg`, and related raster exports.
- Original and project-curated photography in `public/images/`, including orangutans, guides, camps, trekking groups, river activity, food, and Bukit Lawang surroundings.
- Trek, guide, multilingual, testimonial, contact, and practical travel content in `app/site-content.ts`, `app/content/site-text/`, and `app/travel-content.ts`.
- Google Places review integration in `app/google-reviews.ts` and `app/api/reviews/route.ts`; live review availability depends on configuration.
- Booking and PayPal deposit flows in `app/booking/` and `app/api/paypal/`.
- SEO strategy, metadata, localized routes, articles, sitemap, robots, and structured data are already implemented.
- No additional testimonials, certifications, awards, guarantees, customer counts, or conservation impact figures are approved for fabrication.

## Product Principles

1. Make a high-consideration international trip feel understandable and safe to plan.
2. Let the forest, wildlife, local people, and real trip details carry the story.
3. Earn trust through specificity and transparency instead of inflated claims.
4. Guide visitors from inspiration to the right package and a low-friction human conversation.
5. Treat ethical wildlife behavior and local context as part of the experience, not decorative marketing.
